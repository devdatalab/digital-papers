content. Neither the root MIT license nor the CC0 dedication is a blanket license for
all files in this repository or for a paper rendered by the application.

This covers software and its technical documentation, not manuscript text or research

its notices in `examples/nobel/reproduction/package/LICENSE.md`. Imported analysis code
retains its own terms; adapting a paper does not automatically license that code under MIT.

This also covers original introductions explicitly labeled as instructional material

Adapting the starter for a real manuscript does not apply CC0 to that manuscript.

## Research content: no license granted by this starter

All real paper content is excluded from the starter's MIT license and CC0 dedication,
including content added under `papers/` and retained Nobel research material under
`examples/nobel/`. This includes manuscript prose, abstracts, equations, appendices,
figures, tables, captions, notes, source snapshots, PDFs, paper-specific explanations,
and submission correspondence. The exclusion applies in source files, rendered pages,
Markdown exports and downloadable bundles, regardless of file format or location.

publisher. Except where a rights holder has separately granted permission or an explicit
license, all rights are reserved to the extent applicable. Public availability, attribution,
reproduction checks and use of this framework do not themselves grant reuse rights.
This notice does not restrict uses permitted by law or override existing licenses.

Nobel paper prose, original captions and notes, source snapshots and the PDF are included
as reference example material; this starter grants no additional license to them.
Nobel reproduction data retains the terms documented in
`examples/nobel/reproduction/package/LICENSE.md` and

New research inputs and outputs require their own explicit rights review and notices.

## Adapting and publishing a paper

Keep the research-content exclusion when adapting this starter. Record paper-specific
rights in the paper folder and include the relevant notices in its reproduction bundle.
Do not replace it with a blanket MIT, CC0 or Creative Commons grant over a real paper.
Apply a separate research-content license only with explicit authorization from the
rights holders and after checking any journal agreement and applicable obligations.

Before publishing or updating the digital paper, check the intended journal's policies
and signed publishing agreement for the specific manuscript version, hosting location,
required notice and any embargo. Permission to post a preprint does not necessarily
permit posting an accepted manuscript or the publisher's version of record. Excluding
paper text from the starter licenses does not itself establish permission to host it.

Creative Commons licenses and CC0 are generally irrevocable; removing a notice later
cannot undo permissions already validly granted. These exclusions clarify the scope of
this starter and do not revoke any prior valid grants. If research content was previously
released under an open license, disclose that when reviewing a journal's transfer or
exclusive-rights agreement rather than assuming the grant has been withdrawn.
See the [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/),
[CC0 legal terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en), and,
as one publisher's example, [Elsevier's sharing policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/sharing).
