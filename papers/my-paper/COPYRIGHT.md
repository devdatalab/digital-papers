# Paper rights

This folder begins with fictional starter material covered by the CC0 dedication in
[the root licensing scope](../../COPYRIGHT.md). Replacing it with a real paper does not
license that paper under CC0 or MIT. Real paper content and new paper-specific explanations
are excluded from those grants; rights remain with the respective rights holders.

When adapting the template, identify the real paper's title, manuscript version,
rights holder and any journal-required notice here. Do not claim a paper-wide open
license without explicit authorization and review of the applicable journal agreement.
Retain the root research-content exclusion and update
[the bundle notice](reproduction/COPYRIGHT.md) for standalone downloads as well.
